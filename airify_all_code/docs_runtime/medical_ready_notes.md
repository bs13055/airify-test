# Medical-Ready Architecture Notes

## Status: V1 Foundation Only

This document describes what is safe in V1 and what requires future clinical
validation before any medical/health claims can be made.

## What Is Safe in V1

- General environmental guidance ("consider reducing outdoor activity")
- PM2.5 risk awareness (non-medical risk levels: low/moderate/high)
- Action suggestions framed as general environmental guidance
- Non-personalized information
- Clear "not medical advice" disclaimers on all outputs
- Mock/demo data clearly labeled

## What Is NOT Implemented

- Personalized health recommendations
- Medical risk assessment
- Clinical decision support
- Disease prediction or prevention claims
- Validated health prediction models
- User health profile storage
- Medical record integration
- Regulatory compliance (FDA/CE/PDPA medical device)

## Future Compliance Review Required

Before any health/medical claims:
1. Clinical validation of prediction models
2. Regulatory review (medical device classification)
3. PDPA/GDPR privacy compliance for health data
4. Ethics committee review
5. Liability assessment
6. Professional medical advisor review
7. User informed consent (legally binding)
8. Data protection impact assessment

## Privacy Requirements

- No health data stored without explicit consent
- Health profiles (when implemented) must be encrypted
- Right to deletion must be supported
- No sharing of health data with third parties
- Anonymization required for any analytics

## Clinical Validation Requirement

No prediction or recommendation may be labeled as "health advice" or
"medical recommendation" until:
- Validated against clinical outcomes
- Reviewed by qualified health professionals
- Approved by relevant regulatory bodies
- Tested for bias and fairness across demographics
